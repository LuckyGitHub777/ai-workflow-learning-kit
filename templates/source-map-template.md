## Source Evaluation Criteria

Sources are rated on:
- **Credibility:** High (peer-reviewed, official primary, strong methodology) / Medium (reputable secondary with some caveats) / Low (single-source, conflicted, outdated methodology) / Unknown (insufficient information to assess)
- **Recency:** Publication or data date vs. relevance to current question
- **Bias / Conflict:** Explicit or inferred institutional, financial, or narrative bias
- **Methodology / Traceability:** How the information was derived and how easily it can be verified

## Usage in Workflow

This Source Map should be:
- Produced early by the **Source Agent** or Research phase
- Referenced by all subsequent agents (Analysis, Risk, Draft)
- Included in the final artifact or as an appendix
- Updated if new sources emerge during verification

**Golden Rule:** No claim in the final brief may rely on a source that does not appear in this map with a credibility rating.
