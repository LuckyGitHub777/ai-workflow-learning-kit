## Risk Assessment Framework

Risks are evaluated across categories and scored on:
- **Likelihood:** High / Medium / Low (or qualitative probability)
- **Impact:** High / Medium / Low (on mission success, timeline, resources, reputation, ethics, etc.)
- **Second / Third Order Effects:** What else could cascade if this risk materializes?
