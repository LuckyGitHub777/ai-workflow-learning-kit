# AI Workflow Learning Kit

Three reusable blank templates for evidence-based, human-reviewed decisions.

1. `templates/decision-ready-brief-template.md` — define the question, evidence and decision.
2. `templates/source-map-template.md` — document uncertainty and source credibility.
3. `templates/risk-map-template.md` — track risks, mitigations and human approval.

These templates do not connect to accounts, transmit data, trigger autonomous actions or demonstrate measured results. Use fictional placeholders in public examples. Keep clients, private identities, secrets and personal records out of shared material.

The original templates are included with their existing MIT notice. This edition deliberately excludes internal historical commits and operational workflow files. See `MANIFEST.sha256` for exact source hashes.
